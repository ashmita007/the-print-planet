# ID Card Manufacturing Machinery

Heavy-duty production machinery designed for ID card bureaus, school print units, and commercial print shops.

## Technical Specifications
- **Motorized Electric Die Cutter:** Industrial tool-steel die punching CR-80 (85.6 × 54 mm) cards with precision 3.18mm rounded corners at up to 1,200 cards/hour. Single-phase 220V AC.
- **Automatic Heated Fusing Laminator:** Dual heated platens with PID temperature controls (0–200°C) fusing 5-layer PVC prelam assemblies without bubbles or peeling.
- **Manual Slot Punch:** Stamped steel hand tool punching 15 × 3 mm oval slots for lanyard clips.
