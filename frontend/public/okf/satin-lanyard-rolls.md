# Satin Lanyard Ribbon Rolls

Satin lanyard rolls are the primary raw material used by ID card bureaus, corporate gift manufacturers, and digital printing studios to produce customized neck lanyards.

- **Spool Lengths:** 50-meter, 100-meter, and 500-meter rolls.
